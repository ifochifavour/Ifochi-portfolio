About

Background

I'm a Civil Engineer (B.Sc. Building Engineering, University of Lagos) and Graduate Member of the Nigerian Institute of Building, currently working as Assistant Head of the Project Unit and Construction Design Specialist at Dufil Prima Foods.

My work sits at the intersection of design and site delivery: producing construction drawings and technical documentation, preparing accurate Bills of Quantities and cost estimates, running energy audits on industrial facilities, and supervising site teams to make sure what's on the drawing actually gets built to spec — safely and within budget.

I've worked across residential, commercial, and industrial projects — from food processing facilities to road construction and steelworks — and I'm comfortable moving between the drafting table and the site.
